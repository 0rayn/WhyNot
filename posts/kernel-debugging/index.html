I- Debuggin In The Dark: Intro

Imagine this, your favourite bootloader just passed control to your kernel, a brand new kernel, just like a new born baby, can’t do much. heck at least the baby can cry, but for your kernel you have nothing, no drivers means no vga, no text and no sound of course. So how would you know if anything went wrong and what exactly went wrong ?

II- The ABCs: s? S? Serial Communication

<|> The UART Communication Protocol:

One of the most used protocols in embedded devices communication/debugging. And why wouldn’t it. it’s easy to grasp and easy to setup

<|> How does it work ?

In it’s simplest forms I want you to imagine two devices connected with two wires one to transmet data from Device A to Device B and the other to do the inverse of that.

So we only have one wire to send “Hello\n” for example ? how would we do that ? isn’t each character 8 bits ? Yes I hear you, very smart of you to ask this question and the answer is simple, we’ll send each bit one after the other following an internal speed we call the baudrate (how many bits per second). As long as both devices expect the same baudrate we’re good to go :)
